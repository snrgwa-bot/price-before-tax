Privacy Policy

Price Before Tax App

Last updated: October 26, 2026

🔒 Your privacy is important to us. Price Before Tax is designed with privacy in mind — we never collect your name, email, location or anything you enter. If you allow it, the app sends anonymous usage statistics to our own server (you can turn this off in Settings). The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, described below.

Information We Collect

Price Before Tax does not collect any personal information. All calculations are performed locally on your device, and we have no way to access the prices, rates or names you enter into the app. The app may send anonymous usage statistics, described below, and our advertising partner may collect limited data to serve ads, as described in the Advertising section.

Data Storage

Your saved tax rate presets are stored locally on your device using iOS's built-in storage system (UserDefaults). This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us or any third parties
  • Is not backed up to any external servers
  • Can be deleted by uninstalling the app

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features help people and to find crashes, Price Before Tax sends anonymous usage statistics to our own server (hosted on Supabase). This is our own first-party system — no third-party analytics SDK is involved, and it is never used for tracking or advertising. The app identifies itself only with a random installation ID that is created on your device and is deleted together with the app.

What is sent:

  • Device model, iOS version and app version
  • Language, region and time zone
  • Accessibility settings (such as larger text or reduced motion)
  • Session start and end, and the last screen you were on
  • App events, such as “a calculation was completed”, “a result was copied” or “a rate was saved”, with the calculator mode (Before Tax, After Tax, Discount) and a rough tax-rate range — never the prices, amounts or exact rates you enter
  • Remove Ads screen views and which option was tapped
  • Purchase, renewal-status and restore events (the product and plan length only)
  • Review-prompt requests
  • Crash and performance reports from Apple’s MetricKit

What is never sent: your name, email, phone number or address; your location; the advertising identifier (IDFA) or vendor identifier (IDFV); prices paid, receipts or transaction IDs; and anything you type — prices, rates, saved rate names or your calculation history. Your IP address is not stored or used.

Retention: usage statistics are kept for 13 months (crash and performance diagnostics for 6 months), then deleted.

Turn it off: Settings → Privacy → Share Usage Data. When off, nothing is collected or sent, and anything not yet sent is deleted from your device.

Delete it: copy your Installation ID from Settings → Privacy and email it to essentialdevteam@gmail.com; we will delete everything linked to it.

Advertising

The free version of Price Before Tax displays banner ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.

When you first launch the app, iOS asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and you will see non-personalized ads.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), a consent form is shown before ads are served, and you can see non-personalized ads if you decline. You can also purchase the ad-free upgrade to remove ads entirely.

You can manage Google's ad personalization at https://adssettings.google.com.

In-App Purchases

The app offers a Remove Ads subscription that removes all advertising. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see or store your payment details.

Refund requests. When someone asks Apple for a refund of the Remove Ads subscription, Apple may ask us about the purchase. By subscribing, you agree that we may then tell Apple that the purchase was delivered, that a free version with ads was available beforehand, and that we prefer a refund of the unused part. We share nothing else, and only for purchases made after this consent appeared on the subscription screen. We are also notified when a refund is requested or granted, without learning who you are.

Third-Party Services

Other than Google AdMob (for advertising) and Apple's App Store (for in-app purchases), this app does not integrate with any third-party services that could collect your data. Our anonymous usage statistics (above) are our own, first-party system. There are no:

  • Third-party analytics SDKs
  • Social media integrations
  • User accounts or logins

Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. All calculations happen on your device; the only data we receive is the anonymous usage statistics described above. The app is not directed at children under 13; the free version contains third-party advertising as described above.

Data Security

Your prices, rates and calculation history remain on your device and benefit from the security measures built into iOS, including device encryption and secure storage. Anonymous usage statistics are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
